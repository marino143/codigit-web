## English version

```
Your Mac sleeping in the middle of a 2GB download is a special kind of pain.

So is opening Energy Saver settings every time you give a presentation.

I just shipped StayAwake v0.1.1 — a tiny Mac menu bar app that does one thing.

Click it on → Mac stays awake.
Click it off → back to normal.

That's the whole product.

→ Native Apple Silicon (Swift, IOKit)
→ Under 1MB. Launches in <50ms
→ Zero CPU when idle, zero telemetry
→ Free, open source

I built it because I was tired of:
- Long downloads dying overnight
- Zoom calls dimming the screen mid-meeting
- Render jobs interrupted by sleep
- Hunting through System Settings every single time

If "caffeinate from the menu bar" sounds useful to you — give it a try.

Download: https://codigit.hr/apps/stayawake.html
GitHub: https://github.com/marino143/stayawake

This is app #2 of 3 small Mac utilities I'm shipping this month. Native Browser dropped last week. Dock Lock is next.

Feedback, issues, PRs — all welcome.

#MacOS #AppleSilicon #IndieDev #OpenSource
```
