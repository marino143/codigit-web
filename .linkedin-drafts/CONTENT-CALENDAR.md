# LinkedIn Content Calendar — Co Digit Apps Launch

## Strategy

3-week phased rollout. Each app gets a dedicated post, then a recap listicle ties it all together. Spaced out to avoid spamming the feed and to maximize each post's algorithmic lifecycle (LinkedIn shows posts for ~3 days).

**Posting cadence:** 2 posts/week (Tue + Fri). Croatian versions go 3-4 days after EN to a slightly different audience (HR-specific groups, HR-language hashtags).

## Quick-fire checklist before posting

- [ ] Image attached (screenshot or screen recording)
- [ ] Link is the bare codigit.hr URL (NOT a tracking parameter — looks spammy)
- [ ] Hashtags at the end, not mid-post
- [ ] No more than 4 hashtags
- [ ] First 3 lines are the hook (LinkedIn shows ~3 lines before "see more")
- [ ] Engaged with last post's comments before publishing new one
- [ ] Posted from Marino's personal account, NOT Co Digit company page (personal posts get 5-10× more reach)

---

## Important LinkedIn algorithm notes (2026)

- **First hour matters most.** Engage with comments immediately.
- **Native videos beat YouTube links.** Upload directly to LinkedIn.
- **Polls and carousels get boosted.** Use them for variety.
- **Don't edit posts in the first hour** — algorithm penalizes edits.
- **Personal pages > company pages** for reach by ~5-10×.
- **Comments > likes** for reach. Ask questions to drive comments.
