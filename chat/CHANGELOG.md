# What's new

Every update to the chat is listed here, newest first.

## 4 August 2026

- **Emoji reactions** — long-press (or right-click) a message and pick 👍 ❤️ 😂 🎉 👀 ✅ 🔥 🙏. Reactions appear under the message with a count; tap one to add or remove yours, and hover to see who reacted.
- **Reply to a message** — long-press (or right-click) a message and choose Reply. The quote appears above your message, and tapping it jumps to the original.
- **What's new page** — this list, so everyone can see what changed.
- **Pinned conversations** — pin any chat to the top and arrange the pinned ones in your own order. Your arrangement is yours alone.
- **Highlights** — mark important messages with a star and find them all under ⭐, across every conversation.
- **Unread counts fixed** — a window sitting behind other windows no longer counts as "read", so unread badges finally show up. While reading a chat on your phone, the back button shows how many messages arrived elsewhere.
- **Channels marked with #** in the conversation list, groups with 👥.

## 3 August 2026

- **Delete messages** — long-press or right-click a message to delete it for everyone. Your own messages always; other people's only if you own the group/channel or are an administrator.
- **Voice transcripts fixed** — Chrome recordings silently produced no transcript. Voice messages are also much smaller now, which helps on slow connections.
- **File gallery** — the 🗂 button lists every photo, video and voice message shared in a conversation, grouped by type.

## 1 August 2026

- **Staying signed in** — the app used to log you out after about 20 minutes. Sign-ins now last 90 days.

## 31 July 2026

- **Paste and drag & drop** — paste an image with Ctrl/Cmd+V or drag a file into the chat; you get a preview before it sends.
- **Duplicate messages fixed** — messages could appear two or three times on screen, especially on slow connections.
- **Notifications** — each new message announces itself again (they used to replace each other silently), notifications survive browser cleanups, and Settings gained a status readout plus a self-test button.
- **App icon badge** — the number of unread messages now shows on the app icon.
- **Dark mode** — Settings → Appearance: auto, light or dark, per device.
- **Time zones** — see the local time of the person you're chatting with. Your zone is detected automatically.

## 30 July 2026

- **Search** — find any message, including words spoken inside voice messages.
- **Voice messages** — record with the 🎤 button; they are transcribed automatically on the server.
- **Access from anywhere** — the chat moved to its public address, so no VPN app is needed.
- **English interface.**

## 29 July 2026

- **Multiple users, groups and channels** — with an admin page for managing accounts.
- **Push notifications** on phones and computers.
