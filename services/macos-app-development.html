Why native

Swift beats Electron on the Mac.

  • A fraction of the memory. Our ActivityBar runs in ~45 MB of RAM. Typical Electron equivalents idle at 300–500 MB.
  • Instant launch, native feel. Real menus, keyboard shortcuts, drag & drop, and windows that behave the way Mac users expect.
  • Deep OS integration. Menu bar, Notifications, Shortcuts, Keychain, Login Items, Universal Clipboard — first-class, not bolted on.
  • Apple Silicon first. Built and profiled on M-series chips, shipped as universal binaries when you need Intel too.
  • Smaller, safer binaries. No bundled Chromium to patch — less attack surface, faster updates, happier IT departments.

FAQ

Questions US founders ask us.

Do you build in Swift or Electron?

Native Swift and SwiftUI by default. Native apps launch instantly, use a fraction of the memory, and integrate properly with the menu bar, notifications, Shortcuts, and Keychain. If your product genuinely fits Electron better — a large cross-platform web codebase, say — we'll tell you honestly on the first call.

Do you support both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs?

Yes. We build Apple Silicon-first and ship universal binaries that also run natively on Intel Macs when your user base requires it.

Can you handle App Store review, sandboxing, and notarization?

End to end: sandboxing and entitlements, App Store submission and review — or notarized direct distribution with Sparkle auto-updates if you'd rather sell outside the App Store.

What time zone do you work in?

We're based in Zagreb, Croatia (CET), and work a schedule with full-time overlap with the US East Coast working day (8:00–16:00 ET) and UK business hours — real-time standups and code review, not next-day email.

How does pricing work?

A 30-minute scoping call, then a fixed quote within 48 hours. Billed in USD by card or US bank transfer (ACH). Most focused menu-bar utilities ship in 3–6 weeks; larger products get a milestone plan.

Who owns the code?

You do, from the first commit. We sign your NDA before the first call, and all IP transfers to you as part of the standard contract.
