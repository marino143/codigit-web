FAQ

The questions buyers actually ask.

What does nearshore software development actually mean?

Nearshore means outsourcing development to a nearby country in a similar time zone — as opposed to onshore (your own country, highest cost) or offshore (a distant country, cheapest but with a large time gap). The whole point is overlapping working hours: you get answers the same hour instead of the next day.

Is Croatia nearshore for a UK company?

Yes. Zagreb is one hour ahead of London, a two-hour direct flight, and inside the EU and GDPR. In practice it's the same working day with no meaningful time gap.

Is Croatia nearshore for a US company?

Geographically, no — we're offshore from the US, and we'd rather say so than sell you a label. Operationally we behave like a nearshore team: we work a schedule with full-time overlap with the US East Coast day (8:00–16:00 ET), so you get the time-zone benefit of nearshore at European rates.

How is this different from cheaper offshore outsourcing?

Three things. Your working day is covered in real time rather than by overnight handover. You talk directly to the engineer writing the code, not an account manager. And your data stays under EU law with GDPR-grade handling. If price is the only thing that matters to you, offshore will win — we'd tell you that on the first call.

What does it cost compared to hiring locally?

Senior European engineering typically costs a fraction of an equivalent US agency, and sits well below a comparable London day rate. You get a fixed quote in USD, GBP or EUR within 48 hours of the scoping call.

Who owns the code and the IP?

You do, from the first commit. We sign your NDA before the first call, and all IP transfers to you as part of the standard contract. As an EU company we're bound by GDPR for anything touching personal data.

Can you work inside our existing team and tools?

Yes — that's the most common arrangement. Your repo, your board, your Slack, your sprint cadence. We join standups in your hours and review code alongside your engineers.

Need senior engineers who are awake when you are?

Tell us what you're building. You'll get a reply from the engineer who'd do the work — within one business day, in your time zone.
