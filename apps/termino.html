Termino

Online booking for Croatian hair, beauty and nail salons. Clients book in a couple of clicks, 24/7 — owners run the calendar, clients and waitlist from the web or the native iPhone app. Free for salons and clients.

What it does

Small salons still take most bookings by phone — during working hours, mid-treatment, with a paper calendar. Termino gives every salon a public profile clients can book from around the clock, and the owner a dashboard that replaces the notebook.

  • Salon profile with services, staff, gallery, Instagram-style stories and Google reviews
  • Clients book 24/7 without an account — email confirmation and a reminder the day before
  • Day and week calendar, manual bookings and a client base for the owner
  • Per-service daily limits and split shifts — the schedule matches how salons actually work
  • Waitlist: when a slot frees up, the next client is notified automatically
  • Owner push notifications — Web Push in the browser, native APNs in the iOS app
  • Self-serve onboarding — a new salon is live in a few minutes
  • No third-party tracking anywhere; visits are counted first-party, without cookies

Mali saloni većinu termina i dalje primaju telefonom — usred radnog vremena, usred tretmana, s papirnatim kalendarom. Termino svakom salonu daje javni profil s kojeg klijenti rezerviraju 0-24, a vlasniku dashboard koji mijenja bilježnicu.

  • Profil salona s uslugama, djelatnicima, galerijom, storyjima i Google recenzijama
  • Klijenti rezerviraju 24/7 bez registracije — email potvrda i podsjetnik dan ranije
  • Dnevni i tjedni kalendar, ručne rezervacije i baza klijenata za vlasnika
  • Dnevni limiti po usluzi i podijeljene smjene — raspored prati kako saloni stvarno rade
  • Lista čekanja: kad se termin oslobodi, sljedeći klijent automatski dobije obavijest
  • Push obavijesti vlasniku — Web Push u pregledniku, nativni APNs u iOS aplikaciji
  • Self-serve onboarding — novi salon je online za par minuta
  • Nigdje nema praćenja trećih strana; posjeti se broje first-party, bez kolačića

Kleine Salons nehmen die meisten Termine noch immer telefonisch an — mitten in der Arbeitszeit, mitten in der Behandlung, mit Papierkalender. Termino gibt jedem Salon ein öffentliches Profil, über das Kundinnen und Kunden rund um die Uhr buchen, und den Inhabern ein Dashboard, das das Notizbuch ersetzt.

  • Warteliste: Wird ein Termin frei, wird der nächste Kunde automatisch benachrichtigt

  • Self-Service-Onboarding — ein neuer Salon ist in wenigen Minuten online

How it works

The frontend is a static bundle on Netlify — no framework, no build step. Supabase provides Postgres with row-level security, auth and edge functions: transactional email through MailerSend, Web Push over VAPID, and APNs push sent straight to Apple with an ES256 JWT signed in the function itself. Scheduled pg_cron sweeps handle the day-before reminders and review requests.

The iPhone app is the same codebase wrapped in Capacitor — a curated allowlist build ships only the public pages, the App Store binary carries no donation links or consent banners, and a native subclass pins the scroll view so the UI feels native, not embedded. Getting through App Review took four rounds; every rejection made the product cleaner.

Build story

Termino started as a redesign exercise of a local booking product and grew into the full thing: a real marketplace with a live database, email pipeline, push notifications and an iOS app — all on free-tier infrastructure. Apple rejected it four times (a donation button, an iPad layout bug, copy that implied a paid tier, a cookie banner read as a tracking prompt) before approving it in July 2026. Within a month the first salon owner found it organically and signed up on their own. This end-to-end product work — design, backend, App Store — is what I do for founders at Codigit.
