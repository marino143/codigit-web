TideBook

A cycle tracker built for the partner. He sees where she is in her cycle and what actually helps that day, with one tap a day. Nothing leaves the phone.

Download on the App Store → tide-book.com → Privacy policy

Free on iPhone, in English. TideBook Pro is $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year.

Besplatno za iPhone, na engleskom. TideBook Pro košta 2,99 $ mjesečno ili 19,99 $ godišnje.

What it does

Most cycle apps are built for the person whose cycle it is. TideBook is built for the partner who wants to get it right. He enters the date her last period started, and every day the app shows where she is and a few concrete things that tend to help.

  • Today's phase and cycle day, in plain language
  • A short daily playbook: take a chore off her plate, keep plans light, have her snacks within reach
  • A 14-day outlook, so the week before her period doesn't catch him off guard
  • One tap a day, and the forecast moves from the textbook curve toward the couple's own pattern
  • No account and no cloud: everything stays on the phone and can be erased at any time

Većina aplikacija za ciklus napravljena je za osobu čiji je to ciklus. TideBook je napravljen za partnera koji želi to napraviti kako treba. Upiše datum kad joj je počela zadnja menstruacija, a aplikacija mu svaki dan pokaže gdje je ona u ciklusu i nekoliko konkretnih stvari koje obično pomažu.

  • Današnja faza i dan ciklusa, jednostavnim jezikom
  • Kratki dnevni plan: preuzmi joj neki posao, ne pretrpavaj dan planovima, neka joj grickalice budu pri ruci
  • Pregled idućih 14 dana, da ga tjedan prije menstruacije ne iznenadi
  • Jedan dodir dnevno, a prognoza se s prosjeka iz udžbenika pomiče prema njihovom stvarnom obrascu
  • Bez računa i bez clouda: sve ostaje na mobitelu i može se obrisati u bilo kojem trenutku

How it works

TideBook starts from a textbook cycle model and adjusts it as the partner logs a single answer each day, so after a few weeks the outlook reflects the couple's real pattern rather than an average. The free version shows today's phase and one daily tip; TideBook Pro adds the 14-day forecast, the full cycle chart, learning predictions and the complete daily playbook, bought and managed through Apple.

The app is built in React Native with Expo and keeps every entry in local storage on the device. There is no account, no backend and no analytics inside the app. On tide-book.com she can send it to him with one tap, and the privacy policy covers both the app and the site. TideBook is a support tool, not a medical device or a contraceptive method.

TideBook kreće od modela ciklusa iz udžbenika i prilagođava ga kako partner svaki dan zabilježi jedan odgovor, pa nakon nekoliko tjedana prognoza pokazuje stvarni obrazac para, a ne prosjek. Besplatna verzija pokazuje današnju fazu i jedan dnevni savjet; TideBook Pro dodaje prognozu za 14 dana, cijeli grafikon ciklusa, prognozu koja uči i kompletan dnevni plan, a kupuje se i upravlja kroz Apple.

Aplikacija je napravljena u React Nativeu s Expoom i sve unose drži u lokalnoj memoriji uređaja. Nema računa, nema backenda i nema analitike u aplikaciji. Na tide-book.com ona mu je može poslati jednim dodirom, a politika privatnosti (na engleskom) pokriva i aplikaciju i stranicu. TideBook je pomoćni alat, nije medicinski uređaj ni metoda kontracepcije.
