Klik

Screenshots, screen recording and GIFs on your Mac — annotate, then drag straight into any app. 1 MB, native, no subscription.

Download free → View on GitHub ☕ Buy me a coffee

What it does

CleanShot is excellent and costs money every year. Klik is the answer to how much of it you actually use: it sits in the menu bar, grabs a region, a window or the whole screen, records video with system audio and your microphone, and then gets out of the way. Every capture slides into the corner of the screen so you can drag it straight into Slack, Mail, Finder or a browser upload.

  • Region, window and full-screen capture via ScreenCaptureKit
  • Annotation editor — rectangle, arrow, text, highlight, gaussian blur, crop
  • Pin a screenshot so it floats above every other window
  • Screen recording, full screen or region, HEVC with a 1080p ceiling
  • Records system audio and the microphone — built for capturing meetings
  • Echo cancellation and single-track mixing run automatically after each recording
  • Turn any recording into an optimized GIF in one click
  • Captures stack in the corner and drag out into any app that accepts files

CleanShot je odličan i naplaćuje se svake godine. Klik je odgovor na pitanje koliko toga zapravo koristiš: stoji u menu baru, hvata regiju, prozor ili cijeli ekran, snima video sa sistemskim zvukom i mikrofonom, i onda ti se makne s puta. Svaka snimka sklizne u kut ekrana pa je možeš povući ravno u Slack, Mail, Finder ili upload u pregledniku.

  • Snimanje ekrana, cijeli ekran ili regija, HEVC s gornjom granicom od 1080p

Screenshots

Why native?

Klik runs on Apple's own frameworks and nothing else: ScreenCaptureKit for screenshots and video, AVAssetWriter for encoding, ImageIO for GIFs, Carbon Event Manager for global hotkeys. Zero third-party dependencies, so the whole thing builds from a single shell script and ships as a 1 MB .app — a 766 KB binary — not a 150 MB Electron bundle with a Chromium runtime inside it.

Recording targets HEVC with a 1080p ceiling, so a full-screen capture on a 5K display still comes out as a 1080p MP4 — an hour of meeting lands around 1.5 GB instead of needing a re-compression pass before you can share it. System audio and the microphone are captured as separate tracks, then echo-cancelled and mixed down into one AAC track so the file plays properly wherever it ends up.

No telemetry, no analytics, no network code, no subscription. The download is signed with a Developer ID certificate and notarised by Apple, so it opens without a Gatekeeper warning. MIT licensed.

Klik radi na Appleovim frameworkovima i ničemu drugom: ScreenCaptureKit za screenshotove i video, AVAssetWriter za enkodiranje, ImageIO za GIF-ove, Carbon Event Manager za globalne prečace. Nula vanjskih ovisnosti, pa se cijela stvar gradi jednom shell skriptom i isporučuje kao .app od 1 MB, s binaryjem od 766 KB — a ne kao Electron paket od 150 MB s Chromium runtimeom unutra.

Snimanje cilja HEVC s gornjom granicom od 1080p, pa i snimka cijelog ekrana na 5K displayu izlazi kao 1080p MP4 — sat vremena sastanka završi na oko 1,5 GB umjesto da traži dodatnu kompresiju prije dijeljenja. Sistemski zvuk i mikrofon snimaju se kao zasebni zapisi, zatim im se ukloni jeka i spoje se u jedan AAC zapis da se datoteka uredno reproducira gdje god završi.

Bez telemetrije, bez analitike, bez mrežnog koda, bez pretplate. Preuzimanje je potpisano Developer ID certifikatom i notarizirano kod Applea, pa se otvara bez Gatekeeper upozorenja. MIT licenca.

Build story

Klik started as a Saturday-afternoon question: how close can a native app get to CleanShot's core experience with no Electron and no third-party dependencies? The answer turned out to be a single 766 KB binary. Built in Swift with Claude as a pairing partner, MIT licensed, source on GitHub. This is the kind of ship-velocity work I do for founders building SaaS and AI products at Codigit.

Klik je krenuo kao subotnje pitanje: koliko blizu CleanShotovom iskustvu može doći nativna aplikacija bez Electrona i bez vanjskih ovisnosti? Odgovor je ispao jedan binary od 766 KB. Napisan u Swiftu uz Claude kao pair partnera, MIT licenca, source na GitHubu. Ovo je tip ship-velocity rada koji radim za founderse koji grade SaaS i AI proizvode kroz Codigit.
