The Co Digit Manifesto

Keep Moving Forward.

Building software is hard.
Building a business is harder.

Things break. Projects fail. Decisions turn out to be wrong. Technologies change. Clients leave. People leave. Sometimes you give everything you've got — and still lose.

“It ain't about how hard you hit. It's about how hard you can get hit and keep moving forward.”— Rocky Balboa

That's something we believe in at Co Digit.

But moving forward doesn't mean blindly trying the same thing again.

Sometimes you fail simply because you don't know how — yet.

That's why you build your army.

Every book you read adds a soldier.
Every person you learn from adds another.
Every developer you work with.
Every mentor.
Every difficult client.
Every failed project.
Every mistake you never want to make again.

They all become part of your army.

After more than 15 years in software, one thing has become very clear:

Experience isn't knowing all the answers.

It's having enough battles behind you to know what to do when you don't have one.

And when we don't know?

We learn.
We ask.
We experiment.
We find someone who does.

Then we try again.

A little smarter.
A little stronger.
With a bigger army behind us.

Because getting hit is inevitable.

Staying down isn't.

Keep learning.
Keep building.
Keep moving forward.

— Co Digit
